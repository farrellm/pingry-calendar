Notes

Every date is an all-day event in America/New_York, and carries its category in its name. Multi-day breaks are single events, so Winter Break arrives as one band rather than sixteen entries.

The source calendar marks a no-homework evening on Friday 18 September. That is what it prints, so that is what these files say.
